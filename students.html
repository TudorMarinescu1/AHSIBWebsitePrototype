For Students

Build a schedule you can actually live with

IB is rigorous, but it is also flexible. Students can pursue the full diploma, the AHS IB Merit Scholar path, or individual course certificates.

9th

Start foundations

Explore interests, take strong English, math, science, world language, PreIB, Honors, AP, or college-prep courses, and begin thinking about language and math sequence.

10th

Prepare for choices

Continue world language, complete science preparation, and look at which IB subjects fit your goals before junior-year schedules are set.

11th

Begin IB

Start diploma, Merit Scholar, or certificate coursework. Full diploma students begin CAS, Extended Essay, and Theory of Knowledge planning.

12th

Finish and test

Complete senior-year HL and SL courses, register for final exams, finish core requirements, and keep track of college application deadlines.

Student Committee

Use the students who already know the program

The IB Student Committee is student-run and helps coordinate support among students, teachers, parents, and the IB coordinator. The student outreach branch helps current Pre-IB students map out schedules and understand IB and Pre-IB expectations.

The committee also helps with CAS opportunities and keeps students informed about requirements and events.
