Sequencing

Why the two-year plan matters

HL classes generally span both junior and senior year, so they should be treated as a two-year commitment. SL courses are often one-year courses and can help students complete subject groups, balance workload, or explore a specific academic interest.

Some classes have prerequisites or recommended prior coursework. Math placement, science sequence, language level, and arts experience should be reviewed with counselors and teachers before finalizing a plan.

Planning tip

Use the schedule builder after reading the catalog. It automatically places HL classes in both years and lets you add AP or CP courses in the remaining space.
