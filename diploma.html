Diploma Roadmap

How to earn the IB Diploma

The IB Diploma is a coordinated two-year program. Students complete a broad selection of IB subjects, balance Higher Level and Standard Level work, and finish the three-part IB core.

IB Diploma vs. IB Certificate

The IB Diploma requires 6 IB courses total: 3 Higher Level courses and 3 Standard Level courses. Diploma students also complete the core: Theory of Knowledge, the Extended Essay, and CAS.

An IB Course Certificate is for students taking selected IB classes without completing the full diploma. Certificate students can choose the IB courses that fit their goals; they do not need the full 3 HL, 3 SL, and core combination.

AHS also describes an IB Merit Scholar path for students who complete coursework and exams in 3 of the 6 IB subject areas.

Diploma formula

3 HL + 3 SL + TOK + Extended Essay + CAS. Certificate students choose individual IB courses instead of completing the full diploma formula.

Subject Groups

The six academic areas

Diploma candidates plan across the IB subject groups so their program is broad, not narrowly specialized.

Group 1: Studies in Language and Literature

AHS offers IB English Literature HL as a two-year pathway and IB English Literature SL as a one-year pathway. Students build close reading, literary interpretation, discussion, and analytical writing skills.

Group 2: Language B

AHS offers IB Spanish SL, IB Spanish HL, and IB Chinese SL. Spanish SL covers years 3 and 4, Spanish HL covers years 5 and 6, and Chinese SL is listed as a two-year course.

Group 3: Individuals and Societies

AHS offers IB History HL as a two-year course and IB Psychology SL as a focused one-year option. These courses emphasize evidence, research, interpretation, and human behavior.

Group 4: Sciences

AHS offers IB Environmental Systems and Societies SL and IB Sport Exercise and Health Science in HL or SL. Students apply scientific thinking to sustainability, health, performance, and real-world systems.

Group 5: Mathematics

AHS offers IB Math Applications and Interpretation SL for statistics, modeling, and applied contexts, plus IB Math Analysis and Approaches SL for algebra, calculus, logic, and proof.

Group 6: Electives and The Arts

AHS offers IB Film HL, IB Film SL, and IB Music HL. Film supports analysis and production through the FAME pathway, while Music develops theory, history, performance, composition, and listening.

The Core

What sits at the center

Theory of Knowledge

TOK asks students to examine how knowledge is created and evaluated. Students compare ways of knowing such as reason, language, emotion, and perception, then apply those ideas across subject areas.

Extended Essay

The Extended Essay is a 4,000-word independent research paper on a topic chosen by the student and approved by an AHS advisor. It gives students practice with university-style research, drafting, and revision.

CAS

CAS stands for creativity, activity, and service. Students document meaningful experiences beyond class, reflect on their growth, and maintain balance alongside their academic responsibilities.
