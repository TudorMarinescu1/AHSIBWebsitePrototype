Why IB at AHS

A challenging program with a clear purpose

The AHS IB Program prepares students for university-level reading, writing, research, discussion, and exam expectations. Students practice connecting ideas across subjects, examining global questions, and building evidence-based arguments in a setting that values curiosity, balance, service, and intellectual independence.

Juniors and seniors may pursue individual IB certificates or the full IB Diploma. Both pathways can show colleges a willingness to take demanding academic work. The full diploma adds a coordinated two-year plan across the IB subject groups plus the core requirements.

IB students are encouraged to ask challenging questions, learn how to learn, develop strong communication skills, and understand people from different countries and cultures.

HL and SL Courses

What the course levels mean

IB classes are offered at two levels. Knowing the difference makes the diploma plan much easier to read before choosing classes.

HL

Higher Level

HL courses are two-year courses at AHS. They begin in 11th grade and continue in 12th grade, so one HL choice takes space in both years of the schedule.

SL

Standard Level

SL courses are usually one-year courses. Some AHS language SL courses are two-year pathways, so students should check course notes while planning.

Diploma

3 HL courses + 3 SL courses

Full diploma students need three HL courses, three SL courses, and the core: Theory of Knowledge, Extended Essay, and CAS.
