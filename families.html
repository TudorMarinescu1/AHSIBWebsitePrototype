Pathways

Choose the right level of IB

The full IB Diploma requires 3 HL courses, 3 SL courses, and the core: Theory of Knowledge, Extended Essay, and CAS. This path is best planned before junior year because HL classes usually occupy both 11th and 12th grade.

Students can also take individual IB courses for certificates. AHS also describes an IB Merit Scholar path for students completing coursework and exams in 3 of the 6 IB subject areas.

Good family question

Does this two-year plan leave enough room for sleep, activities, arts, athletics, service, and college applications?

Parent association

The AHS IB Parents Association supports IB students, teachers, and the school through advocacy, fundraising, information sharing, volunteer support, and community building.

Exam costs and registration

IB exam registration happens through the school. Juniors usually register for SL courses they are completing, with language exceptions; seniors register for courses they are taking or finishing.

College credit

Universities set their own IB policies. The UC system may award units for a completed IB Diploma with qualifying scores and for individual Higher Level exam scores.
